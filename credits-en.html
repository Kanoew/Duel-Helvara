Duel d'Helvara

The game

Universe, characters, texts and art direction: Kanoew. Game mechanics: a location-and-energy card game in the spirit of digital card games with simultaneous turns. Only mechanical ideas are shared; the names, visuals and universe are those of Duel d'Helvara.

AI-generated artwork and music

  • The illustrations of cards, locations and packs were generated with artificial intelligence tools, from descriptions written by the author. They may draw on artists' styles without reproducing any existing work.
  • The music was created with Gemini (Google), from descriptions written by the author.
