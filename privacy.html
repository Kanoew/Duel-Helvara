Privacy

Duel d'Helvara · updated October 12, 2026

Duel d'Helvara is a free card game made by an individual (Kanoew), with no ads and no real-money purchases. This page explains what data the game keeps, why, for how long, and how to delete it.

What is stored

DataWhyWho can see it
An account ID (random). As soon as you open the game, an "anonymous" account tied to your device is created.To save your game.It is reachable through the leaderboard and by connected players (see below), but it reveals nothing about you.
Your progress: cards, decks, in-game currency, rank, quests, match history.To give it back to you on each visit.Only you.
Your leaderboard entry: the nickname you choose, your avatar (a card illustration), your rank, your season stats and the time of your last game (used only to show an "active" badge for 10 minutes).To display the leaderboard.All players.
If you sign in with Google: your e-mail address and your Google account ID.To find your game again on another device.Only you. Your address is never shown to other players. The game does not receive your contacts, your Drive or your e-mails.

Card statistics: at the end of a game against the AI, a Blitz or a duel (not the tutorial), for each card you played: its name, the location where you played it, the game result (win, loss or draw), the mode and the day. This data is added to counters.To balance the cards.The developer only. It is linked to no account or nickname, so it cannot be traced back or deleted one entry at a time.

To prevent abuse, a technical counter tied to your account keeps only the time of your latest submissions (feedback, statistics, leaderboard): it does not contain what you sent and disappears with your account.

The game uses no advertising cookies and no analytics. Choose a nickname that does not identify you: it is public.

The live lobby

Duels use live channels, opened only when you create or join a room, or look for an opponent (never when you play against the AI or look at the leaderboard). A room with a code is a channel shared by its two players; the opponent search uses a channel that gathers the players searching at the same time. In these channels, the other players present see your account ID and, for the length of the search or the match: a random token and the game version (search), or your nickname, avatar, deck and room code (duel). Your rank and stats are not broadcast there. Matches have no chat: no free text is exchanged.

For how long

  • Anonymous account (no Google): deleted after 12 months without signing in, or after 14 days without signing in if it has not finished a single game.
  • Google account: kept until you delete it, and deleted after 24 months without signing in.
  • In every case you can delete your account whenever you want (see below).
  • The copy kept in your browser stays until you clear the site's data.

Where the data is, and who has access

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles accounts. The server is located in Ireland, in the European Union.
  • Google is involved only if you choose "Sign in with Google".
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site: like any host, GitHub sees visitors' IP addresses.
  • Fonts and music are served by the site itself: no request is sent to Google Fonts or to an ad network.
  • The Discord button opens another website with its own policy: nothing is sent to it from the game.

Google and GitHub may process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards set by their own terms. Nothing is sold, rented or used for advertising.

Storage in your browser

The game keeps in your browser (local storage, no tracking cookie): your progress, your language, your sound settings and the sign-in session. This is needed for the game to work, so there is no consent banner.

Your rights: see, export, delete

  • Export: in the game, Settings → "Export my data" downloads your progress as a file.
  • Delete: in the game, Settings → "Delete my account" erases your account, your progress and your leaderboard entry, immediately and for good.
  • Correct: you can change your nickname and avatar at any time in Profile.
  • Any other request (access, objection, reporting another player's nickname): write to Kanoekayak57@gmail.com, or open a request on the project page without including personal data.

You can also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL) if you believe your rights are not respected.

Age

The game is meant for players aged 15 and over. Below that age, ask a parent before playing or signing in with Google.

In-game currency

The in-game currency ("CHF") is earned by playing. It has no real-world value and cannot be bought or sold.
